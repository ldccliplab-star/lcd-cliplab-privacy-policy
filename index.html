Privacy Policy

LCD ClipLab

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

LCD ClipLab provides tools to create, manage and publish video clips to connected social media accounts. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

2. Information We Access

When you connect a social media account, LCD ClipLab may access information made available through that platform's authorization process and APIs. This may include account identification, profile information, channel information and permissions needed to publish content.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the application, connect your accounts, manage your video clips and perform publishing actions that you request.

4. Social Media Platforms

Connections to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok are subject to the respective platforms' terms and privacy policies. LCD ClipLab accesses these services through their authorized APIs and only uses the permissions granted during account connection.

5. Sharing of Information

LCD ClipLab does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by connected platform services as necessary to perform actions you request, or where disclosure is required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Disconnecting Accounts

You can disconnect a social media account through the application's connected-app settings. You may also revoke the application's access through the relevant social media platform's security or connected-app settings.

8. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information associated with your use of LCD ClipLab, contact us at the email address below. Please include enough information for us to identify your request. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: ldccliplab@gmail.com
